# Trance factory-bank research and sample sources

Research date: 13 November 2026. These are independently authored, track-inspired sounds, not original artist patches, licensed recreations, extracted record stems or measured identical reproductions. References describe musical roles and sound families; where original settings are unavailable, synthesis settings are our own. No song melodies are bundled.

## Technical and artist sources

- **R_JP:** [Roland JP-8000 original manual](https://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/JP-8000.pdf). Super Saw Detune/Mix informed the distinction between unison spread, centre/body balance and stereo width. Used with SPACE INFINITY's independent oscillators, unison controls, filters and envelopes; its DSP is not a JP-8000 emulation.
- **R_FERRY:** [Ferry Corsten: Out of the Blue](https://djmag.com/watch/system-f-out-blue-making-trance-classic-watch) and [his essential studio gear](https://www.djtimes.com/2020/02/ferry-corsten-essential-studio-gear/). Support the JP-8000/supersaw reference. The DJ Mag page was search-indexed but full access was restricted; no exact settings were extracted.
- **R_COSMIC:** [Cosmic Gate interview](https://trancesound.wordpress.com/2010/07/23/interview-with-cosmic-gate-south-africa-feature/). Artists mention Virus use; exact oscillator/filter settings for the classic tracks are not established by this interview. Metallic FM/warp, octave layers and fast contours are authored interpretations.
- **R_PICOTTO:** [Mauro Picotto interview, Soundwall](https://www.soundwall.it/mauro-picotto-come-reinventarsi-nel-corso-degli-anni/) and [artist interview](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/music/interviews/Mauro%2BPicotto%2BInterview-65057.html). Sampling, feedback and individual sound invention are relevant; no claim that our Lizard/Iguana/Komodo inspirations reproduce his original settings.
- **R_COCOOMA:** [Cocooma discography](https://lacargo.de/cocooma/) and [track catalogue](https://www.beatport.com/artist/cocooma/138845/tracks). Verified reference titles include Virtual Experience, District of Power, Rage, Flying Saucer, The Escape, The Yellow Base, Energetic, Metronoom, Blaues Wunder and Nothing Is Over. No reliable original patch sheets were located. The German lead section uses deliberately different pulse, FM, sync, vowel, fifth and octave designs rather than asserting a common original instrument.
- **R_TB:** [Roland TB-303 original manual](https://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/TB-303_OM.pdf), [Roland Cloud TB-303 manual](https://www.rolandcloud.com/CMS/getmedia/b3e2da28-1951-467e-bd79-7aae34481570/TB-303-Manual-E.pdf?ext=.pdf), and [Roland TB-303 history](https://www.rolandus.com/blog/2012/12/03/synth-legends-tb-303/). Saw/square sources, resonant low-pass, envelope decay, accent and overlapping-note slides inform the acid bank. Velocity drives cutoff and level; our analogue filter is not represented as a circuit-identical 303.
- **R_STRINGS:** Gordon Reid's [string-machine synthesis](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/synthesizing-strings-string-machines) and [PWM strings](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/synthesizing-strings-pwm-string-sounds). Ensemble unison/chorus/PWM informs the analogue string designs, distinct from recorded violin/cello sections.
- **R_CHILDREN:** [SynthMania famous sounds](https://synthmania.com/famous-sounds/), [Children retrospective](https://www.insomniac.com/music/from-the-crate-robert-miles-children/), and [Robert Miles interview, 1997](https://www.stuartbuchanan.com/archives/1997/11/27/interview-robert-miles-married-with-children/). The dream-house reference calls for piano with space and strings, not the generic supersaw recipe in the pasted guide. We use a legally redistributable grand piano recording, not Kurzweil ROM data or the original recording.
- **R_SAND:** [Darude interview](https://funktasy.com/exclusive-interviews/darude/) and [Before the Storm artist interview](https://www.musicradar.com/news/classic-album-darude-on-before-the-storm). Darude identifies a distorted tracker sample for the Sandstorm lead; the JP-8080 Sandstorm preset was a pad. [Syntorial's saw/pulse +31-semitone recipe](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/darude-sandstorm-lead/) is useful as a **remake technique**, not evidence of original recording provenance. Desert Blade is a synthesis interpretation, not a recovered sample.
- **R_ALONE:** [Syntorial Better Off Alone recipe](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/alice-deejay-better-off-alone-lead/). Simple saw, immediate attack, sustained body and short release inform Silver Alone. The page's JP-08 original-hardware attribution is chronologically implausible and is not repeated here.
- **R_ATB:** [Syntorial ATB 9PM recipe](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/atb-9pm-till-i-come-lead/). Vocal/guitar-like pluck and bend articulation inform Twilight Meow; source recordings are not copied.
- **R_BASS:** [Ørjan Nilsen bass walkthrough](https://musictech.com/tutorials/step-by-step-bass-prod-with-orjan-nilsen/) was available as a search summary, full-page access restricted. Filter-envelope punch and clean sub/upper-harmonic separation are general synthesis decisions; no exact settings attributed to this restricted source.
- **R_PATTERN:** Original root-relative arpeggiator/sequence patterns authored for this product. Ten arpeggiators and ten 16-step sequences; references indicate genre energy only. All 35 bass patches and all ten acid patches have their note generator disabled.

## Imported recordings and rights

- **R_VCSL:** [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), [CC0 licence](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/blob/master/LICENSE), pinned source commit `c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`.
- **R_VSCO:** [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/), [source library](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), [CC0 licence](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE), pinned commit `440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e`.
- **R_VOICE:** Existing independently recorded [SpectMorph voices](https://github.com/swesterfeld/spectmorph-instruments/blob/master/README.md), CC0; processing provenance remains in `native-vst3/Samples/SAMPLE_PROVENANCE.json`.

The new 42-zone grand/upright/violin/cello/spiccato/pizzicato import has source blob verification, source/output SHA-256, processing details and effective MIDI roots in `native-vst3/Samples/Trance/PROVENANCE.json`. The reproducible import script is `scripts/prepare-trance-samples.py`; the pinned selection is `scripts/trance-sample-selection.json`. Roots were checked against measured pitch: these libraries use different octave-name conventions. Velocity layers and nearest-key selection are built into the native synth. Sustain strings use an internal loop with a crossfade so holding a note does not repeatedly trigger its bow attack. CC0 permits commercial redistribution, including embedding recordings in a VST.

Real string sections, synthetic ensembles and the FM dream-key piano are explicitly distinguished in the preset catalogue. Multisamples improve realism, but no "perfect replica" claim is made.
