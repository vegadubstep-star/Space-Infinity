# SPACE INFINITY 0.6.3 — bass voicing update

All 35 bass presets have been rebuilt: 15 offbeat stabs, five sub stabs and 15 rolling basses. They follow your own MIDI notes, without a sequencer, gate or pump. The other 125 sounds retain their previous designs and settings, including the 0.6.2 acid cutoff fix.

The offbeats now cover saw/octave punch, rubber pulse, FM knock, detuned saw body, octave weight, vocal pulse, driven razor, metallic bounce, rounded triangle, octave snap, fifth body, FM steel, drawbar weight, resonant acid body and warm low-pass. The rolling bank uses corresponding, tighter articulations with shorter filter contours and firmer amp sustain. The five subs are pure sine, triangle warmth, filtered pulse, restrained FM harmonics and octave sine body.

Clean saw/pulse/sine/triangle foundations replace the shared morphed starting point. Oscillator phase and mono routing keep the low end repeatable; deliberate octave/fifth/FM layers separate the roles. Amp envelopes retain body behind the transient. Selective tube, transistor, diode and clipped colours add upper harmonics through a high-passed wet branch, while the dry blend preserves bass weight. Effects remain dry and focused rather than adding reverb or stereo wash.

TONE opens or enriches the body, PUNCH shortens articulation and CHARACTER adds controlled harmonic drive. The five sub presets stay clean by default, with a longer amp body and no pitch kick. Notes around MIDI36–55 are a useful starting register; the sub bank is voiced an octave lower internally.

Preset levels are measured after voicing. Both held-note auditions and actual bassline renders are checked for headroom. New tests exercise all35 sounds for mono compatibility, body after the initial attack, clean note endings and twelve repeated-note levels. Identical MIDI old/new bassline renders support comparison without relying on a volume increase.

The oscillator/sub mixer and separate filter/amp articulation were checked against [Roland's SH-101 manual](https://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/SH-101_OM.pdf) and [SH-101 synthesis guide](https://articles.roland.com/start-your-synthesis-journey-with-six-legendary-roland-cloud-instruments/). These are authored trance/hard-trance interpretations; they are not claimed as recovered original artist patches or measured hardware models.

Close FL Studio, extract the installer ZIP and run Setup. Select the revised factory bass sounds to compare: existing saved projects and user replacements retain their saved patch settings.
