# Trance / hard-trance review references — 18 November 2026

Read online for this review:

- https://theproducerschool.com/blogs/music-production/best-hard-house-trance-serum-presets-2025-complete-guide — separate amp/filter envelopes; shorter filter articulation for bass, restrained drive, seven-semitone layers, octave-up supersaw support at reduced level, velocity-driven cutoff, and restrained delay/reverb. This is a tutorial, not an original-hardware specification.
- https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/synthesizing-plucked-strings — picking position changes harmonic notches, the initial rounded string profile reduces high partials, and the spectrum/decay depend on excitation and resonances. This supports distinct resonator/comb/pluck colours rather than twenty identical saw plucks.
- https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/darude-sandstorm-lead/ — saw/pulse layering and metallic upper-register lead character. Interpret with original spectra; do not copy factory assets.
- https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/atb-9pm-till-i-come-lead/ — articulating a guitar-like pluck through pitch bend and filter shaping. Treat secondary claims about original synthesis architecture cautiously; the JD-990 is sample based.
- https://www.cableguys.com/volumeshaper — phase/beat-locked shaping, multiband ducking, stepped/curved/ramped rhythms, and smoothing to avoid clicks.
- Official Serum2 manual https://xferrecords.com/manual/serum-2/docs, pp190–192 and345 — shape menu is an LFO preset loader, and .XferShape files are shared with LFOTool; these are distinct from OSC wavetable sources. The owner's clarification requests OSC sources. Use original harmonic generation rather than redistribute Xfer tables.

Original M1/JP8000/Nord2 architecture references already inspected are recorded in HERITAGE_200_RESEARCH.md. The new sources preserve existing oscillator indices and add parabolic/skew/trapezoid/step/odd-even harmonics, layered saw/pulse, resonator/reed, FM-edge and comb colours with editable frame position. No claim these are Serum's proprietary factory tables.

Review workflow: retain stable preset IDs and assets; simplify browsing roles while keeping substyle tags. Compare rendered sound and articulation across both banks, including cross-bank candidates. Measure BS.1770 perceived loudness, true peak, repeated notes, chords and macro extremes; a common peak alone is insufficient. Pluck OPEN macro opens cutoff and sustain while reducing wet delay/reverb and adding restrained width/drive. Sampled one-shots retain their instrument's decay rather than promising an impossible sustained oscillator tone.

## Makina follow-up

Read https://www.happyhardcore.com/forums/topic.asp?topic_id=23381 (2005 production discussion) and https://www.happyhardcore.com/forums/topic.asp?topic_id=54246 (2011 layered-hardcore lead tutorial), plus https://www.rewiredrecords.co.uk/products/makina-toolkit-5 (original pack producer page). The forum discusses saw/square layering, detune, fast articulation, controlled high-rate pitch/FM texture and moderate post-processing. Its guesses about Vanguard/Pro-53/JP8000 use are community opinions, not verified Skudero or Sistema 3 studio inventories. Rewired separates leads, hoovers, stabs and basses, supporting a broader palette than one supersaw template. Artist-specific searches found Makina Legends/Skudero reference sets and a Skudero/Metralla interview; those were discovery results, not analysed audio/video, so do not present them as extracted artist settings.

Eight original Makina interpretations use pulse, trapezoid, reed/FM, harmonic-stack and layered-saw cores with distinct unison/mono/glide/articulation, short wet tails and TONE/RAVE/EDGE macros. They remain in the Lead role, with a Makina tag. No third-party artist patches, samples or MIDI were copied. Steady choir/hybrid colours for the Cocooma references are a separate part of the palette.
