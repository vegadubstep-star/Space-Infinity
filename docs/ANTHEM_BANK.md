## Synth support

- **Pitch** is appended to the modulation destination list. LFO curves are centred around the played pitch for this destination; routing ±1 depth gives up to ±4 semitones. Static macros and amp-envelope routes retain their own bipolar/unipolar input. The new leads use much smaller values. LFO ON bypasses the pitch route as well as the other routes. This destination acts on synthesized voices, including their sub.
- **ENGINES → ANALOGUE → MIDI bend range** sets the incoming keyboard pitch-wheel range from 1–24 semitones. Default ±24 preserves earlier behavior; Twilight, Halo, Solar and Frostline select ±2. The existing on-screen PITCH parameter is still a direct semitone control over its existing ±24 range. Wheel movement retains the existing smoothing.
- Each oscillator's **WARP** popup now includes an additional **semitone offset** alongside its octave and fine tune. Desert uses +2 octaves and +7 semitones. Defaults of zero preserve existing sounds.

Saved states keep their raw destination indices; expansion of a destination-selector menu can change how old normalized host automation selects its entries. Macro amount automation is separate from this caveat.

Native audio checks cover the new melodic patches' repeated-note level and state recall, and measure the two-semitone MIDI bend, 31-semitone oscillator interval and bipolar pitch modulation from real output zero crossings. WAV previews are generated by the lead checks. Sparse arp and companion parts are also covered by the full bank audition; FL Studio playing and final timbre still need the owner's listening test.

## Aurelia Growl Bass (F146)

An independently generated three-frame vowel/reed/saw table FM-modulated by an inaudible pulse oscillator, three A voices with restrained spread and a clean sub. A quarter-note LFO moves table position, mid-band cutoff and A's FM amount together. The voice band-pass focuses the vocal midrange; post EQ supplies a 34 Hz 24 dB high-pass. This is a vocal-growl interpretation of the supplied guide, not a claim that it reproduces a specific Gouryella recording. Moderate pre/post drive, parallel multiband compression and a very quiet short delay provide edge.

**Osc 1 Warp** and **Osc 2 Warp** are appended modulation destinations, available in the routing controls and by dragging a source to the WARP buttons. They add up to half the full warp range at depth 1. Existing patches have no routes to these destinations. The new patch sets A's base FM to 18% and adds up to 10% through its LFO.

### FM and phase controls

Each oscillator WARP popup now offers **FM from Sub** and a **warp envelope** selector (Off / Amp / Filter). The sub's sine phase is generated even when Sub level is zero, so it can modulate without adding audible low end. Warp envelope scales that oscillator's warp amount using the existing independent amp or filter envelope. This supports the Alloy stab's two distinct FM sweeps without fabricating separate third/fourth envelopes.

**RETRIGGER PHASE**, also in each WARP popup, resets its oscillator and unison phases at a retriggered note-on. Defaults are off, preserving free-running behavior in existing sounds. It does not retrigger an already connected legato voice, consistent with legato articulation. Foundry and Undertow enable it for consistent note starts. Native checks compare repeated audio attacks, test sub FM with no audible sub, and measure Undertow's mono energy.

Amp ADSR, filter ADSR and pitch-envelope attack/decay now use rotary hardware-style knobs with numeric values. The amp envelope keeps its LONG x3 timing switch, graph editing, automation and existing parameter identities. The analogue/performance page uses a matching knob grid.
