# SPACE INFINITY 0.9.3

Preset arrows and the header selector follow the saved/manual bank order,
anchored to the last selected sound. A–Z is an explicit BANK action.

IMPORT CLEAN preserves the imported WAV's stereo image, bass and level at its
root note, and removes inherited filtering, colour and effects. Previous saved
patches retain their original decoding and gain behaviour. New sampled patches
also preserve stereo through the primary granular engine.

+ OSC B adds an independently controlled oscillator to a sampled instrument.
Sample gain, octave and filter routing remain independent. One-shot completion
does not cut off the oscillator layer; GRAINS retains access to granular controls.

Portamento now finishes in the displayed time using a smooth semitone curve.
The knob offers finer short times, Shift adjustment and typed ms/s. Mono glides
across short gaps; Legato glides on overlapping notes. A new phrase after a pause
starts at its played pitch.

Preset selection and opening other dropdowns dismiss unrelated edit/assignment
panels. Hidden or closed editors dismiss their panels as well.

Heritage 200, Lush Trance 20 and Orbit Voices install automatically, preserving
local edits. Orbit Voices contains 48 sample-based sounds: 12 choir variations,
21 leads and 15 basses, using 40 renamed INFINITY WAV sources. New sounds include
OPEN, SPACE and BODY macros. The audio audit records level and true-peak checks.
Seven confirmed close Heritage variants are archived from the default list;
portable backups retain them without shifting existing factory IDs.

Factory replacement exports can be uploaded for a later build. For extra-bank
edits, use SAVE AS to export portable .sipreset files and ZIP them together.
Preset names, creators, tags, settings and embedded samples travel with the file.

See READ_ME_FIRST.md for installation and control checks, and audit/ for native
test, level and curation evidence. Audio checks cover the documented notes,
chords and macro extremes; arbitrary edits or denser chords can change peaks.
