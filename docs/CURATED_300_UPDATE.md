# Curated 300 — a focused installed palette

The normal browser contains 300 sound presets plus Init. Bass, Lead, Pluck,
Pads, Strings, Piano & Keys, Choir & Voices, Arps & Sequences and the smaller
Acid, Organ, Mallets, Textures and Percussion sections keep the bank easy to
scan. All included sounds and their sample data load automatically. No manual
bank import is needed.

The previous 421-sound palette was rendered at three registers and compared
using sustained spectrum, attack spectrum and amplitude contour. The closest
redundant alternatives were removed within musical roles. This is broader
curation than deleting byte-identical files: some removed alternatives differ,
but add less to the focused palette. The curation audit records each removed
sound and its closest retained alternative. Audio-feature similarity cannot
promise perceptual uniqueness for every melody, macro setting or listener.

Every retained chain has a recorded effects, articulation, warp and unison
review. Conservative edits clear excessive wet tails and low-frequency buildup
in returns while keeping the dry fundamentals. Selected acid variants use
filter-envelope-linked PWM, sub FM or mirror warp for different harmonic
behaviour. Classic unwarped acid voices remain. Bass attacks and near-zero lead
attacks retain their programming. Existing performance routes remain usable.

The sampled leads and basses have their source onsets inspected. Revised start
positions preserve the original transient instead of cropping a fixed
percentage from every file. Choir patches retain their selected steady segment;
loop crossfades remain enabled where a sample is looped. Source WAV bytes and
root pitches are unchanged. Final levels and overload checks are measured after
these edits, including full performance macros and chords at three registers.

The browser opens with a large sound list, a category selector, independent tags,
search, CLEAR and a visible result count. Click a sound to load; after typing a
search, Enter loads the first result and Escape clears the filters. List arrows
select a sound and Enter loads it. Filtering alone does not change the sound.
EDIT / SAVE reveals creator/tags, save/replace/export and hybrid controls. Header
preset arrows continue from the loaded sound in saved/manual order.

Old project states and personal replacements are preserved. Untouched factory
alternatives are hidden from the normal list. An optional
SPACE_INFINITY_Curated_300_Alternatives.zip contains the 121 newly removed
alternatives; importing it deliberately adds recovery entries. The earlier
seven-sound recovery bank remains available. Recovery ZIPs are not required to
use the main 300 sounds and are not installed into the normal list.
