# Factory choir recordings

- Orbital Choir Ah uses [Choir.aif by DiArchangeli](https://freesound.org/people/DiArchangeli/sounds/458144/), CC0 1.0.
- Ethereal Choir Ooh uses [Voice Choir OOOH.wav by Uzbazur](https://freesound.org/people/Uzbazur/sounds/413713/), CC0 1.0.

The WAVs are edits of compressed previews. Edits include excerpting, tuning,
high-pass filtering, fades, and level matching. The Ah recording is a chord.

### 0.5.0 multisampled pianos and string sections

Sam Gossner / Versilian Studios — VCSL and VSCO 2 Community Edition, CC0-1.0. 42 derived zones, including grand/upright piano and bowed/plucked strings. See `Trance/PROVENANCE.json` and `THIRD_PARTY_SAMPLES.md` for pinned sources, hashes and processing. No artist record samples or proprietary instrument ROMs are included.
